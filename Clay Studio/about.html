About

Clay studio is a small independent business that collects design of clays, potters and includes ceramics that allow people to buy and sell their creations, ideas and knowledge

Founded by a small local artist, the studio began as a garage workplace and has grown into studio space.

By 2023, the studio opened its doors to beginners through weekend pottery classes, welcoming anyone curious enough to try centering clay for the first time. That same spirit of openness and craft continues to guide the studio today: things made slowly, meant to be used and loved for years.
